Privacy Policy

How ConnectiveStack handles information when you visit our website or use ConnectiveStack OS.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Who we are and scope

ConnectiveStack operates connectivestack.com and the ConnectiveStack OS business software at app.connectivestack.com. This notice covers our website, inquiries, and the software, including its closed-beta features. Contact hello@connectivestack.com for privacy questions or requests. For customer workspace data, the customer may determine the purposes of processing and we handle the data to provide the service on their instructions.

2. Information we handle

  • Account details, such as name, email address, authentication identifiers, workspace membership and preferences.
  • Business records you enter or import, including contact and company details, notes, opportunities, tasks, communications and related activity.
  • Website inquiries, project requirements, browser and device information, usage and diagnostic logs, and cookie or storage preferences.
  • Authorized integration data, including account identifiers, selected permissions, access and refresh tokens or API credentials, and the records retrieved or submitted using a connected account. The exact data depends on the provider, permissions and enabled features.

Current integrations and planned capabilities differ. We do not receive a provider's data merely because its name appears in the app. We access it only when the relevant connection and feature are authorized and enabled.

3. Google and Meta platform data

For Google services, we use authorized account identifiers and relevant email, calendar, document, spreadsheet, analytics or Search Console data where the corresponding feature is enabled. ConnectiveStack OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI models or serve advertising.

Meta integrations are under development. When supported features are enabled and you authorize them, the app may access Facebook or Threads account identifiers, business and advertising asset identifiers, campaign configuration, advertising performance and spending metrics, or Threads profile and content data, as permitted by the scopes you approve. Additional Facebook Page or Instagram capabilities require separate supported permissions and review where applicable. We use Meta Platform Data only for the requested business management, reporting, content or advertising functions allowed by Meta's policies. We do not sell Platform Data, use it for unrelated profiling, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. We do not request your Facebook password. Advertising changes or publishing are not authorized merely by connecting an account.

4. Why we process information

We use information to provide requested features, authenticate users, organize workspaces, synchronize authorized records, respond to inquiries, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, maintain service records and comply with applicable law. Where applicable, processing relies on providing the requested service, your authorization or consent, legitimate operational and security purposes, or legal obligations. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, without affecting prior lawful processing.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use Cloudflare for hosting and delivery and Supabase for authentication and database services. Email delivery, scheduling, analytics and authorized third-party integrations may involve their respective providers. Data may be processed outside your country. We limit sharing to what is necessary for the requested feature, service operations, security or legal requirements. We do not sell personal information. Optional AI and other third-party services may process inputs necessary for a feature you choose to use, subject to applicable provider restrictions and the permissions granted. Restricted integration data must not be transferred to an AI provider unless permitted by the originating provider's rules and your authorization.

6. Storage, security and retention

We use HTTPS, authenticated access, database access controls and encrypted storage of supported integration credentials. No system is perfectly secure. Do not send passwords, private API keys or access tokens by email. We retain information while needed to provide the service, resolve issues, meet contractual or legal obligations and protect against misuse. When data is no longer needed, we remove or de-identify it, subject to necessary legal or security retention and backup lifecycles.

7. Cookies and analytics

The website uses browser storage for preferences and may use analytics to understand website performance. The application uses session and other necessary storage for sign-in, security and authorized integrations. Website cookie preferences can be managed through the website footer; browser controls may also be used. Disabling necessary storage may affect sign-in or other features.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, export, deletion, restriction or objection to processing, and withdraw applicable consent. You may also have the right to complain to a relevant data protection authority, including the Philippine National Privacy Commission where applicable. We may verify your identity and authority before acting on a request. For customer-controlled workspace records, contact the workspace owner as appropriate. See our data deletion instructions for provider-specific or account-wide requests.

9. Children and changes

The software is intended for business users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal data. We will update this notice as features or processing practices change and identify the revision date on this page. Contact us at the email above with concerns.
