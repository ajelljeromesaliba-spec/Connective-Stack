Data Deletion Instructions

Request deletion of your ConnectiveStack OS account, workspace data or connected provider data.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Choose what to remove

You can request removal of a specific connected provider's data (including Facebook or Threads data), selected personal information, or your entire ConnectiveStack OS account. Disconnecting a provider or revoking its permissions prevents future authorized access but does not necessarily remove data previously stored in the software. Request deletion separately if you want that data removed.

2. Send a deletion request

Account deletion is currently handled by support, not an automated self-service button. Email us from the address associated with your account:

Request data deletion by email

Email: hello@connectivestack.com
Subject: ConnectiveStack OS Data Deletion Request

Include the email used for ConnectiveStack OS, the provider or account to remove (for example, Facebook or Threads), and whether you want provider-only deletion or full account deletion. If you manage multiple workspaces, identify the relevant workspace. Do not include passwords, access tokens, app secrets or unnecessary identity documents.

3. Remove Meta permissions if connected

You may also remove ConnectiveStack OS from your Meta account's authorized apps or business integrations, as applicable, through your Facebook or Threads account settings. Facebook provides an Business Integrations settings page and Apps and Websites settings page. Sign in to the relevant account and remove ConnectiveStack OS if it is listed. Interface names may vary. Revocation does not delete your Facebook or Threads account or content stored by Meta itself.

4. Verification and processing

We will verify your identity and authority, clarify the scope if necessary, and process valid requests without undue delay, in accordance with applicable law and provider terms. We remove the relevant stored authorization credentials and data within our control, subject to necessary legal and security exceptions. For a full account deletion, we also review account-associated records and access. Shared workspace records may be controlled by the workspace owner or subject to other users' legitimate rights; we will coordinate accordingly. We will confirm the result and explain any applicable retention exceptions. Backup copies may remain until their normal expiration and must not be restored for ordinary use after deletion.

5. Export or other privacy requests

If you need a copy of your information, request it before deletion. If you cannot access your registered email, contact support and we will discuss a safe alternative verification method. Read our Privacy Policy for further details.
